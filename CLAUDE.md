# Mountain Connect

Seasonal worker platform connecting workers with ski resort businesses worldwide.

## ⚠️ Read AEO.md before any SEO, AEO, content, schema or stats work
[AEO.md](AEO.md) holds the answer-engine strategy, the ChatGPT baseline, the
honesty rules for any published figure, and — most usefully — **what the data
can and cannot support**. Three commonly-wanted figures do not exist in the
database (housing cost, whether housing is deducted from pay, and town), so
checking it first is faster than discovering that halfway through a page. It also
records the 2026-10-10 decision to KEEP JobPosting JSON-LD on unclaimed
listings, which is ~98% of the board — so `occupationalCategory` is a claim
made to Google and a wrong category is a wrong claim. Keep it updated as
things ship.

## Supabase Patterns
- **User-scoped client** (`createClient` from server.ts or client.ts): respects RLS, use for user-facing reads
- **Admin client** (`createAdminClient` from admin.ts): bypasses RLS, use for cross-user operations (notifications, conversations, admin actions)
- **RLS is enforced** on all tables. Admin policies in migration 00011. **Verified by probe on 2026-08-19**, not merely asserted: anonymous callers, plus throwaway authenticated worker and business accounts with no relationships, were pointed at all 24 sensitive tables and at storage. Everything user-scoped returned 0 rows (`worker_profiles`, `applications`, `messages`, `interviews`, `contracts`, `audit_logs`, `outreach_leads`, `support_reports`, `login_otp_codes`, `expressions_of_interest`, `nfc_taps`, …); `users` returns the caller's own row only; `business_profiles` and `job_posts` are public by design. Storage: an authenticated stranger cannot list the resumes/contracts/documents buckets, cannot list another user's folder, and cannot sign another user's file, while public buckets still serve. **Two exceptions found:** `conversation_participants` raises 42P17 (infinite recursion) so it has NO working policy — it fails closed, and the app only works because `/api/conversations` uses the admin client throughout; and `lead_posts` returns 403 by design (no policy, service-role only). Re-run the probe after any policy change — and note that RLS also lives in dashboard-created policies that never appear in `supabase/migrations/` (see 00090), so reading the migration files is not a substitute.
- **Realtime** enabled on `messages` table only

## Key Database Tables
- `users` — auth users with role (worker, business_owner, admin)
- `worker_profiles` — worker details, skills, availability, contact_email. ⚠️ `preferred_job_types` holds ROLES — the profile editor's `JOB_TYPE_OPTIONS` ("Hospitality", "Lift Operator", …); full-time/part-time/casual is `position_type`. Onboarding wrote `"full_time"` into the roles column until 00099, where the editor could not remove it.
- `business_profiles` — business details, verification_status, resort_id, nearby_town_id (source of truth for where the business is — trumps the resort link; auto-stamped from `location` text by trigger added in 00074. Legacy `operates_in_town` flag kept for backward compat but no longer gates display logic)
- `business_venues` — establishments under each business (added in 00076). One row per venue, with name/slug/description/location/resort_id/nearby_town_id/logo/cover/contact + an `is_primary` boolean (one primary per business, partial unique index). The primary venue mirrors the business's own data and is auto-created for every existing/new business. Verification is at the business level, not per-venue.
- `job_posts` — listings with nearby_town_id, venue_id (FK to business_venues, nullable for backward compat. ⚠️ **"populated for every active job since 00076" is no longer true**: venue_id is NULL on all 355 open listings as of 2026-10-10, because the board is now almost entirely scraped imports and the import route never sets it. Anything relying on the venue — including the exact town for a business with premises in two towns — has to fall back to the business), how_to_apply, application_email/url
- `applications` — worker applications to jobs
- `interviews` — scheduling with status (invited, scheduled, completed, cancelled, missed, reschedule_requested)
- `resorts` — 111 resorts with legacy_id text field for backward compat (UNIQUE constraint on legacy_id added in 00072)
- `nearby_towns` — 50+ towns with 30+ detail fields
- `resort_nearby_towns` — many-to-many join
- `conversations`, `conversation_participants`, `messages` — messaging system
- `notifications` — in-app notifications with types
- `saved_jobs`, `job_alerts` — worker features

## Business Registration Flow
Businesses can post listings regardless of verification state. Verification is a trust signal, not a gate.
1. Sign up (with resort selection) → email confirmation
2. Onboarding creates business_profiles with `verification_status: "pending_review"`
3. Business can immediately create + publish active job listings (previously draft-only). Their jobs and profile are publicly visible right away.
4. Admin reviews at `/admin/registrations` → approve/reject/request info
5. On verification: status → "verified", green "Verified" badge appears on their profile + job listings + the employers directory; celebration shown
6. Unverified businesses still show publicly but with an amber "Not yet verified" note on their profile page so workers know the business hasn't been vetted
7. Admins can verify/unverify any business at any time from `/admin/businesses`

## Important Conventions
- Resort `id` in database is UUID. Static data uses `legacy_id` (text: "1", "2", etc.)
- Resort FK lookups in migrations: `(SELECT id FROM resorts WHERE legacy_id = '52' LIMIT 1)`
- Town slugs are lowercase hyphenated: "whistler-village", "hirafu-kutchan"
- `snow_reliability` CHECK constraint: only "high", "medium", "low" (NOT "moderate")
- Email from address: `Mountain Connect <notifications@mountainconnects.com>`
- **Emails must render the same in Outlook for Windows**, which uses Microsoft Word's engine and silently drops CSS it does not know. Never write `background:linear-gradient(...)` — write `bgcolor="#hex"` on the `<td>` plus `background-color:#hex;background-image:linear-gradient(...)`, where `#hex` is the gradient's DARKEST stop (every gradient here carries light text). No `rgba()` except inside `box-shadow`, no flex/grid, no `background-image:url()`, no `<style>` blocks. On 2026-09-18 the shorthand blanked every header and button in 44 of 45 templates on a Lenovo. `lib/email/templates/email-compat.test.ts` scans the whole directory — a NEW template is checked the moment it is saved — and CI runs it via `.github/workflows/test.yml`. Rounded corners and shadows are allowed; Outlook just ignores them.
- **Numbers a public page quotes about our own size come from `lib/stats/platform-stats.server.ts`, never from a literal.** `/about` shipped in April with "69 Ski Resorts", "12 Countries" and "50+ Mountain Towns" typed into the JSX — twice over, plus a fourth copy inside a sentence — and by September the real figures were 111, 14 and 89. A literal cannot go stale loudly, so it understated the platform for five months with nobody noticing. The module is split in two ON PURPOSE: `platform-stats.ts` holds the type, `EMPTY_STATS` and `formatStat` and is safe for client components; `platform-stats.server.ts` holds the query. Importing the server half from a client component drags `next/headers` into the browser bundle and 500s the route — **typecheck does not catch it, only rendering the page does**. `formatStat` renders an unavailable count as "—", never 0 and never a remembered figure. Any page using this needs `export const dynamic = "force-dynamic"` (see the sitemap note in 57fb081: `revalidate` still prerenders at build time, where the Sensitive Supabase keys are withheld).
- **Every email goes out multipart, and outreach is capped at 50/day.** Two deliverability fixes from 2026-09-22. (a) `withTextPart()` in `lib/email/client.ts` derives a `text/plain` part from the HTML of every outgoing message, via `lib/email/html-to-text.ts`. All 45 templates were HTML-only, which is a bulk-sender signature — ordinary mail carries both parts. It lives in the SEND PATH, not in the templates, so the next email anyone writes gets one without having to remember; a caller that passes `text` explicitly keeps its own. Verified by rendering every template and confirming the converter introduced no problem the HTML did not already have. (b) `lib/outreach/pacing.ts` holds `DAILY_OUTREACH_LIMIT = 50` over a ROLLING `PACING_WINDOW_HOURS = 23` window (a calendar day would allow 100 across 23:59 and 00:01; **23 and not 24 because the cron fires at 10:00 UTC and its sends land at 10:00:13 — a 24-hour window measured from the next run's start still contains them, so the budget reads as spent and the run sends nothing, alternating 50/0/50/0 while looking like it works.** Caught on 2026-09-25 from the real send times, before the second run; a test now pins the window below the cron interval) shared by `/api/admin/outreach/leads/bulk-send` and `/api/cron/outreach-drip` — one domain, one reputation, one budget. What does not fit is **deferred, not dropped**: bulk-send returns those leads as `skipped` with a message saying they go on the next run, and the cron takes the longest-overdue first. ⚠️ It fails CLOSED — if the count query breaks it assumes the day is spent, because a deferred batch sends tomorrow while a spike cannot be recalled. Why it exists: the 2026-09-21 run put 180 emails on the wire in one second, and set 209 follow-ups to fall due in the same minute two days later — which the cron would have sent as 209 sequential API calls, past the Resend rate limit that once cost a campaign all but five of its deliveries.
- **Resend open and click tracking stay OFF, and outreach clicks are measured with UTMs instead.** Open tracking is an invisible 1x1 image; Apple Mail Privacy Protection pre-fetches it for every recipient, so the resulting "open rate" measures mail clients rather than interest. Click tracking rewrites every link through a redirect domain. Both are signals filters weigh against unsolicited mail, and cold outreach already carries a 3.9% bounce rate (00104) on a domain shared with transactional mail and Tyler's Workspace inbox. `lib/outreach/cta.ts` is the alternative: every outreach CTA is built by `businessSignupCta(template)`, which tags the signup link with `utm_source=outreach`, `utm_medium=email` and the TEMPLATE in `utm_campaign` (campaign, not content, because campaign appears in GA4's default acquisition reports). It also sets `src=outreach`, which `/signup` reads through `lib/campaigns/attribution.ts` and writes to the new account's auth metadata, so an outreach-driven business signup is countable in the database as well as in GA4 — alongside the 00073 trigger that flips a matching lead to `signed_up` on business insert. Five send paths build that link and all five go through the helper; `lib/outreach/cta.test.ts` pins the shape AND asserts that attribution.ts's `SOURCE_PATTERN` still accepts the `src` we send, since that check silently drops anything it dislikes.
- **Test mode is development-only.** Under `next dev`, `?test=true` or a `test-mode=true` cookie lets a logged-out browser into the portal shells, which fall back to demo data; in production every portal route needs a session (`middleware.test.ts`). Until 2026-09-19 production honoured the cookie too — an unsigned `true` anyone could set by hand, so the code `/api/test-portal` asked for (a literal fallback in this public repo) never guarded anything. There is no site-wide access gate — it was removed 2026-04-04, and nothing reads the `site-access` cookie `/api/access` still sets.
- **Email confirmation links go through a button, never a bare GET.** Supabase's "Confirm signup" and "Reset password" templates (Dashboard → Authentication → Emails) must link to `https://www.mountainconnects.com/auth/confirm?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&type=email` (reset: `&type=recovery`). Loading `/auth/confirm` uses nothing up. Its button POSTs to `/api/auth/confirm`, which calls `verifyOtp` and then routes through `lib/auth/path-after-sign-in.ts`, shared with `/auth/callback`. Why: the default `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}` is spent by its first GET. On 2026-09-19 Tyler's own test signup was confirmed by a mail scanner 15 seconds after the email was sent, so his click got `otp_expired` and "Authentication failed". It is also a PKCE link, which only completes in the browser that signed up, and ad signups never are: they sign up inside Instagram's in-app browser and confirm from Gmail or Safari. In both cases the email IS confirmed, so `/login?error=auth_failed` now explains that instead of claiming failure. A login attempt with an unconfirmed email gets a 403 `email_not_confirmed` from `/api/auth/login`, and the page offers a new link. `/auth/callback` still serves Google OAuth and any links sent before the template change. **Template status: switched by Tyler on 2026-09-19**, both "Confirm signup" and "Reset password". ⚠️ **Never proven end to end:** no real signup has run through the new page. His retest reused an address that already had an account, so Supabase sent nothing and he clicked the original 4:42 email instead, and he declined further tests. The first real ad signups are the proof — each should show `email_confirmed_at` with a `last_sign_in_at` moments later and a `worker_profiles` row. Accounts that sit unconfirmed for a day point at the template link, not at the code.

## Job import pipeline

The Facebook monitor (`scripts/fb-monitor/`) scrapes the groups in `groups.json`, extracts each post with Claude, and posts to `POST /api/admin/job-listings/import`. **Notion is no longer part of this** — it was removed on 2026-08-31 and `job_posts.notion_id` was renamed to `import_key` in migration 00096, because every value in it comes from the scraper (`fb-<hash of group+business+title>`) and never had anything to do with Notion. The endpoint still accepts `notionId` as an alias so an older checkout of the scraper keeps working.

**Idempotency, two lookups in order:** `import_key` first, then **business + title** (case-insensitive). The second exists because the FB importer derives the key from *(group, business, jobTitle)*, so one advert cross-posted to two groups hashes twice — Seasons Niseko produced a duplicate of every role that way, 20 pairs. On a match the row is **updated, not inserted**: status is preserved (a re-import cannot revert a published listing to draft) and absent fields are left alone (a thinner re-scrape cannot erase richer data).

**Cutting the extraction bill.** Extraction is the only paid part (~$0.011/post, ~$113/mo measured over 16 runs). Three free gates run before it, cheapest and most certain first:

0. `scripts/fb-monitor/ledger.ts` — posts already extracted, unchanged. A post sits near the top of a group's feed for days and is re-collected nightly; 32% of a recent sample had been seen before, and 758 hiring detections over a fortnight produced only **79 net-new listings**. Keyed on post id **plus a hash of the text**, so an edited post re-extracts automatically — that is what makes it lossless rather than a tradeoff. Only SUCCESSFUL extractions are recorded, so a failed night retries. 30-day TTL, stored at `~/.mountain-connect/extracted-ledger.json`.

1. `scripts/fb-monitor/prefilter.ts` — keyword rules. Replayed over 688 real posts: drops 16%, loses **zero** listings the model called hiring. ⚠️ Order matters: any hiring signal wins outright, because "accommodation" is both the top rejection reason AND in most genuine ski ads ("staff accommodation provided").
2. `scripts/fb-monitor/triage-local.ts` — a free local model (Ollama, qwen2.5:7b) answers one question: does this post offer work? Off unless `FB_LOCAL_TRIAGE=1`. Measured on 250 held-out posts: 58% of what reaches it dropped, **3.6% of jobs lost** (~6 listings/month once duplicates are accounted for). Enabled on all three launchd agents via the generator in `install.ts`; anything it cannot answer is SENT, so a broken Ollama degrades to today's behaviour.

⚠️ `scripts/fb-monitor/extract-ollama.ts` does the FULL extraction locally and is a last resort only — measured at **11 jobs lost in 30**. The difference is the schema, not the model: asked seventeen fields under constrained decoding a 7B model gets the substance wrong, asked one question it nearly matches Opus. Do not switch extraction to it to save money.

**Businesses are found-or-created by email via `lib/admin/business-by-email.ts`.** Several rows may legitimately share one address — Odin Living's recruitment inbox advertises Odin Living, Mūsu Bar & Bistro and The Barn by Odin — so the lookup asks for a LIST and picks: a row whose name matches the incoming post wins, else the claimed row, else the oldest. An unrecognised name falls back to the canonical row rather than inserting. ⚠️ The two cases that must never be conflated are "no rows" (insert) and "the query failed" (abort): the previous `.eq("email",…).maybeSingle()` returned null data plus a discarded PGRST116 error whenever two rows matched, which read as "no such business" and inserted a duplicate — eleven Odin rows in a week, cleaned up in 00097. Email matching still cannot see past a *different* address for the same company — Odin Living arrived as three records (`recruitment@`, `hrmanager@`, and a misspelled `recuritment@`) and each was emailed separately. `lib/admin/duplicate-businesses.ts` now flags likely duplicates in `/admin/jobs` on three signals — shared non-free email domain, identical punctuation-stripped name, and an email within two edits of another. It flags only; merging is a judgement call (00095 merged Odin by hand). ⚠️ Free email providers are excluded from the domain signal or every gmail address becomes one cluster.
